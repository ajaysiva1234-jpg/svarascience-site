The method

CLAP: Chunk, Lock, Arrange, Perform

A memorization method that puts information onto a Carnatic rhythm cycle instead of a melody. No instrument, no singing, no music training needed. Just rhythm. Free for anyone to use.

C
ChunkBreak material into small groups of 3–5 items.
Pi becomes 314 · 159 · 265 · 358 instead of one long string.
L
LockSet each group to one rhythm cycle.
Each group gets its own beat, so the rhythm holds its place in line.
A
ArrangePut the hardest item on the strongest beat.
The group you keep forgetting goes on sam, the first beat, where every cycle lands.
P
PerformSay it aloud with the beat.
Clap or tap the cycle and speak each group on its beat.

Try CLAP in 30 seconds

Tap once per beat. Each beat locks three digits of Pi in place. After one full cycle, hide the digits and say them back with the rhythm.

What CLAP is built for

Ordered sequences: digits, lists, and steps that have to stay in order. That is where rhythm's fixed slots fit most naturally.

Open questions

Does it work for words or full paragraphs? Does it help people understand and use material, not just repeat it? These are still to be tested.

Honest status
